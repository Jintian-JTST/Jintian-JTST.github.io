Academic Interests

My current interests include computational physics, particle and high-energy physics, numerical modelling and simulation. I am particularly interested in parameter estimation, uncertainty and the comparison of simplified models with experimental data.

Alongside physics, I explore sensing and interaction through Unity, computer vision and hardware prototypes.

Shanghai Jiao Tong University · July–September 2026

PSXM Magnet Design

As a summer research student, I modelled a six-coil magnet using Python/NumPy and least-squares current solves. I examined dipole and quadrupole fields, compared numerical results with an ideal-shell reference and studied shield-radius trade-offs within a 2D approximation.

I co-authored a 19-page technical report with Zejia Lu under Professor Liang Li and presented the study to SJTU–KEK collaborators.

Research project · April 2026

Muon g-2 Toy Monte Carlo

I analysed simulated decay-positron time spectra using a five-parameter fit, compared a saved fit with the known input frequency and examined toy and ROOT-data residuals with Fourier analysis.

The work combines scientific Python, model fitting and uncertainty analysis, documented in a 12-page technical report.
