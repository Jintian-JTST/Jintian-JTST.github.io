Jintian Wang / JTST

I'm an MPhys Physics student at Exeter College, University of Oxford.

I started programming in 2016. My projects now combine that interest with physics: writing numerical models, testing them against measurements and building interactive systems.

Enrolled October 2025 · Expected completion June 2029
First-year result: Distinction (2025/26)

Academic interests

I'm interested in computational and particle physics, particularly how simplified models can be checked against experimental data. Parameter estimation, uncertainty and residual structure have been recurring questions in my research work.

Most of my numerical work uses Python, NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib, alongside Git and LaTeX.
