President, 14th Oxford China Forum

Oxford China Forum · July 2026–present

The Oxford China Forum, founded in 2013, is a student-run academic forum at Oxford on China-related questions across disciplines. Over thirteen editions it has hosted more than 300 speakers, and since 2018 its sessions have been broadcast on platforms including Bilibili, Sohu, iQIYI and Phoenix TV.

As President of the 14th forum, I coordinate the volunteer team across external relations, publicity, content and operations.

AI salon: How does AI think?

9 September 2026 · Chongqing · with the College of Media and International Culture, Zhejiang University

The forum's summer special event, part of an Asian young leaders salon, asked where the boundary lies between asking AI to retrieve information and asking it to make a judgement. The afternoon moved from model mechanisms to practice inside organisations.

Programme

Opening remarks · keynote on deploying models inside organisations · a multimodal test of human–AI collaboration skills · roundtable on AI in enterprise practice. Speakers included Hao Yunqi (Vice President, 58.com Group), Zhao Yupei (Associate Dean, College of Media and International Culture, Zhejiang University) and Wu Yizhen (founder of Otterlantis).
