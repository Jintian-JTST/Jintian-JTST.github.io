OneTapReality

Travel-album app · React Native / Expo · TypeScript · Front-end contributor

OneTapReality is a four-person project for creating, editing and sharing travel albums, with an NFC gift-card workflow. The app is in external iOS beta on TestFlight.

The idea is simple: travel photos usually end up buried in the camera roll. The app turns them into a small album you keep editing, and an NFC keepsake can open that album again later. Its Chinese name is 一触如初.

On the team page I go by 大虚. I work on the front end; a teammate leads backend development.

01 /

How the app works

Make a draft. Pick photos and fill in a title, city and date. The app lays them out as an editable album draft. The generator only uses that metadata and the photo order; it does not look at image content.

Edit it. Move photos, change text and rework the layout on a canvas, with 80+ stickers, 40+ frames and 40+ background textures bundled in the app.

Share it, if you want to. Albums stay on the device by default. Only when the owner signs in and publishes an NFC gift are that gift's snapshot and chosen photos uploaded, so the person holding the card can open it.

A city check-in and footprint map sits alongside the albums; a city lights up only when you record it yourself, with no automatic location tracking.

Status, as of late 2026: the album editor, footprint map and NFC flow are in the iOS beta, and the first batch of NFC keepsakes is with early testers. Physical city-flower souvenirs, a shop and AR features are still concepts.

My Front-End Contributions

Album editing: refined the canvas editor's interactions, including font controls and photo handling, so layouts could be edited, saved and reopened.

Draft recovery: added explicit recovery and discard choices for unsaved album edits, including recovery of title and travel-date metadata.

Persistence: fixed saved rotation state and photo/font persistence across editor and reader views.

Account transitions: guarded asynchronous editor updates against changes of account or editing session, and added regression checks for draft recovery, geometry and account changes.
