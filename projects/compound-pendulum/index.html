Liquid-damped compound pendulum

How does liquid drag affect the period of a compound pendulum? This study compared two drag models with oscillations measured in water.

Modelling · Experiment design · Python data analysis

01 /

Models and experiment

I derived and compared descriptions with linear and quadratic liquid drag, using small-angle approximations to model the oscillation period.

I designed the experimental setup with 3D-printed components, varied the mass distribution and rotational radius, and measured oscillations from video. Python analysis compared the measured periods with the model predictions.

Scope and uncertainty

The comparison concerns the radii and experimental conditions investigated in this project. The small-angle approximation and uncertainty in timing and apparatus dimensions limit the interpretation of agreement between a model and the measurements.

The work is documented in a 21-page IPQ research report.
